Chapter 1: Salvage Sector Seven

The hull of the derelict cruiser groaned as Aria's magnetic boots locked onto its scorched dorsal plating. Beyond her visor, the rings of Veyra-9 turned silently against a sea of amber dust.

"Power signature is definitely alive," muttered her wrist-comm AI, Pip. "And it's humming in an encryption dialect older than the Concordat."

Aria smiled behind the frost-rimmed glass and ignited her plasma cutter.

Chapter 2: The Foundry Wakes

As the bulkhead swung inward, ten thousand dormant amber beacons ignited down a cathedral-sized bay filled with half-assembled starships no human shipyard had built in five centuries.

A calm holographic voice echoed through her helmet: *Welcome back, Chief Forgemaster. Production queue stands at zero. Awaiting first blueprint.*
